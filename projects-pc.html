Custom PC Build

Researcher & Builder

Hardware PC Building Research Budgeting
Overview

Because of my interest in CAD and 3D modeling, running software like Blender and Fusion 360 on my laptop was no longer practical—it would constantly overheat and crash under heavy loads. Recognizing my passion, my dad gave me a budget of $1,500 to design and assemble a dedicated workstation capable of handling demanding CAD workloads.

The condition was that I had to research, source, and construct everything myself. I spent my winter break diving deep into computer architecture—evaluating chipsets, component compatibility, thermal headroom, form factors, and future-proofing options. Beyond yielding a high-performance system for my daily engineering tasks, this build provided invaluable hands-on experience in hardware systems engineering.
