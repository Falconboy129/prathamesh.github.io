Engineering Motivation

Upper-limb prosthetic abandonment rates range between 26% and 45% due to rigid control methods[cite: 8]. Standard commercial prosthetics rely on fixed, factory-calibrated thresholds that cannot adapt to natural variations in human neuromuscular signals caused by muscle fatigue, sweat, or shifting user conditions[cite: 8]. When a rigid system misfires or delays, the resulting frustration often leads to complete device abandonment[cite: 8].

Additionally, commercial upper-limb myoelectric devices cost between $10,000 and $100,000+, placing them far out of reach for most amputees globally[cite: 8]. This project demonstrates a functional, accessible biomedical alternative that achieves personalized control using an on-device machine learning model for under $300[cite: 8].

System Architecture & Signal Pipeline

The system uses a modular, distributed architecture that separates signal acquisition, intelligent intent interpretation, and mechanical actuation to ensure real-time responsiveness and fail-safe operation[cite: 8].

1. sEMG Signal Acquisition

Surface electromyography (MyoWare) sensors attached to the residual limb detect electrical activity produced by muscle contractions, translating physiological intent into raw voltage signals[cite: 8].

2. ESP32 Preprocessing

An ESP32 microcontroller acquires raw sEMG data in real time, applies baseline signal filtering, and validates data integrity for safety before passing stream packets to the central computing unit[cite: 8].

3. On-Device Random Forest AI

A Raspberry Pi performs feature extraction—specifically Mean Absolute Value (MAV) and Root Mean Square (RMS) calculation[cite: 8]. An in-house Random Forest classifier maps these EMG signatures to intended grip patterns in milliseconds without requiring constant manual recalibration[cite: 8].

4. Pulley & Tendon Actuation

Validated movement commands trigger five MG 996P servo motors[cite: 8]. Each servo drives a custom pulley wheel connected to a high-strength string tendon system, replicating natural finger flexion and extension[cite: 8].

Hardware Implementation

Modular Mechanical Frame

The hand, palm, and forearm chassis are 3D-printed with internal mounting channels[cite: 8]. The modular structural layout ensures individual hardware elements or actuators can be serviced without replacing the entire arm[cite: 8].

Power Delivery & Fail-Safe Control

High-torque servos are isolated on dedicated power buses with signal validation steps implemented between the Raspberry Pi decision loop and ESP32 PWM drivers to eliminate erratic motor behaviors[cite: 8].
