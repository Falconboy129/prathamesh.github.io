Overview

Mechanical Mages is a non-profit organization and FTC robotics team (Team 20288) competing across Sunset, Westview, and Jesuit High Schools in Portland, Oregon. Beyond competing, the organization is focused on spreading robotics awareness in the community — running FLL summer camps for elementary schoolers, hosting mock FLL tournaments, and organizing official FLL tournament events. I co-founded the team and lead all mechanical design, CNC fabrication, and strategic direction.

Custom Differential Claw Deposition SubSystem

Click to Expand ▼

A multi-servo deposition system mounted on horizontal slides featuring a claw with 2 degrees of freedom for sample and specimen deposition.

Fusion 360 CNC Linear Motion

Build Timeline & Iterations

• Iteration 1 (League Meets 0–2): Utilized a rotating arm attached to vertical slides to pick up samples from the intake tray. While functional, it took 3 seconds to rotate 180 degrees and struggled with picking up specimens (cycle times over 30 seconds).
• Iteration 2 (Post-League 2 to States): Redesigned into a claw mounted on horizontal slides with a compact wrist and dual-servo rotation to increase torque, maintain speed, and secure specimen clipping onto the high bar. Cycle times dropped to under 7 seconds.

5in Ball Diameter Shooter

Click to Expand ▼

A dual-motor launcher using a flywheel mounted on a ball bearing turret with an adjustable hood to manipulate release angle.

3D Printing Flywheel Physics Iteration

Design Criteria & Iterations

Design Criteria: Shoot from anywhere on field, achieve 95%+ accuracy, and fire all balls in under 1.5 seconds.

• Iteration 1 (League 0): Used the starterbot shooter setup, requiring about 5 seconds to fire three balls.
• Iteration 2 (League 1): Switched to an adjustable hood shooter mounted on a bearing stack turret and added a Limelight camera to track the goal, reducing shooting speed to 3 seconds.
• Iteration 3 (League 2): Switched to smaller wheels and altered motor positions for compactness while maintaining the 3-second firing rate.
• Iteration 4 (Qualifiers): Integrated heavy steel washers as flywheels to increase inertia, lowering the rate to 0.5 seconds per ball.

Pocketed Aluminum Chassis

Click to Expand ▼

Parallel plate dead axle chassis milled from 1/8in 6061 aluminum sheets with custom weight-reduction pocketing.

Fabrication CNC Machining 6061 Aluminum

Build Timeline & Iterations

• Iteration 1 (League 0): Built the standard GoBilda mecanum strafer chassis with rookies for team training.
• Iteration 2: Switched to a belt-driven wooden chassis for compact layout, rapid prototyping, and cost efficiency in partnership with Portland Community College's makerspace.
• Iteration 3: CNC-milled 1/8" 6061 aluminum plates featuring strategic pocketing for structural strength and weight reduction.
