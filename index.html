About Me

I am a senior at Sunset High School completing both the Engineering and Computer Science pathways alongside a rigorous IB curriculum, while concurrently taking advanced computer science courses at Portland State University. As Co-Founder and Team Captain of my robotics team, my technical skills span advanced 3D CAD design in Fusion 360, hands-on digital fabrication (operating CNC machines, laser cutters, and 3D printers), software development, and custom PC engineering.

As Co-Founder and Captain of Mechanical Mages (FTC Team 20288)—a registered non-profit—I lead competitive engineering initiatives and direct community outreach programs. My work includes hosting FLL summer camps for elementary students, organizing official FLL tournaments, and collaborating with global and local robotics organizations to expand STEM accessibility. Outside of engineering, I am a competitive athlete—ranking in the Top 20 for Oregon varsity racquetball and holding the 2024 Oregon Open Badminton Championship (Men's Doubles)—as well as a pianist.
